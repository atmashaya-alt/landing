About us

Engineered for wealth, legacy, and community.

We're an independent technology platform built on one belief: every South African deserves world-class financial advice — no matter their life stage, income, or background.

Partridg3 is community-first. We connect you with independent, FSCA-licensed advisers who aren't captured by any single insurer or product house — free to recommend the right product from any provider, because their only loyalty is to you. Partridg3 itself is not a Financial Services Provider and does not give financial advice.

Independent Advisers & Tax Practitioners
Individually licensed · FAIS Act compliant · FSCA / SARS registered

Every adviser and tax practitioner on Partridg3 is independently licensed and personally vetted before their profile goes live. Each operates under their own licence — not on behalf of Partridg3 — bringing expertise across life, disability, income protection, retirement, estate and tax planning.

Independent collaborators are individually licensed under the FAIS Act (No. 37 of 2002) and regulated by the FSCA of South Africa, or registered with SARS as tax practitioners — not employed by or acting on behalf of Partridg3.
