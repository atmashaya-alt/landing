What Partridg3 is

Partridg3 (Pty) Ltd is a technology platform. We are not ourselves a Financial Services Provider (FSP) and do not give financial advice. We connect you with independent advisers who are individually licensed under the FAIS Act (No. 37 of 2002) and regulated by the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA). No money passes through Partridg3 — we exist to educate and connect, not to transact.

Adviser independence

All financial advisers on the Partridg3 platform are independently licensed FSPs. Through this platform, your adviser has access to a wider professional network — including specialist advisers and industry experts — so that your financial planning draws on the best available knowledge in the industry, not just one adviser's view. Any financial advice you receive is provided solely by your licensed adviser in their personal professional capacity, and not by Partridg3.

No advice from Partridg3 itself

Information, wellness scores, gap analyses, and financial needs analyses displayed on this platform are for general educational and illustrative purposes only. They do not constitute financial advice as defined in the FAIS Act and should not be relied upon as such.

Calculators & tools

All tools and calculators on this site produce estimates based on the information you provide. They do not reflect your actual or guaranteed financial position, are not guaranteed accurate, and do not constitute financial advice. A licensed adviser will do a full needs analysis with you before any recommendation is made.

Use of AI

All financial planning on this platform is human-led. Artificial intelligence assists with communication, scheduling, and administrative tasks only — it does not plan, recommend, or make decisions on your behalf. Every recommendation and financial plan you receive has been reviewed and is owned by your licensed adviser, not by any automated system.

Acceptable use

You agree to use this platform, and the calculators and forms on it, for their intended purpose — getting an estimate of your own financial position and, if you choose, requesting a consultation with an adviser. Submitted information should be accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Limitation of liability

Partridg3 provides this platform "as is." We do not guarantee the accuracy of calculator outputs, and we are not liable for decisions made based on illustrative estimates rather than a full, licensed financial needs analysis. Liability for financial advice given rests with the individually licensed adviser who gave it, not with Partridg3.
