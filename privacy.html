Privacy Policy

Working draft. This policy is compiled from Partridg3's existing internal compliance disclosures (the same POPIA/FAIS notices shown across our client and adviser platforms) and has not yet been reviewed by an attorney. Treat it as an accurate starting point, not final legal sign-off.

Who we are

Partridg3 (Pty) Ltd is a technology platform — we are not ourselves a Financial Services Provider (FSP) and do not give financial advice. We connect you with independent advisers who are individually licensed under the FAIS Act (No. 37 of 2002) and regulated by the Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA).

Information we collect

We collect what you give us directly — through our consultation request form, this is your name, WhatsApp number, email address (optional), and the product you're interested in. We don't collect this information silently: it's only submitted when you fill in and send the form yourself.

We currently use no third-party analytics or advertising tracking scripts on this site — no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, nothing that profiles you as you browse.

How we use it

  • To connect you with a licensed adviser who can contact you about the product(s) you expressed interest in.
  • To respond to you directly if you message us via WhatsApp or email.

We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for anything beyond the adviser-matching purpose you submitted it for.

Sharing with advisers

When you submit a consultation request, your details are shared with a licensed adviser on our platform so they can contact you. Any advice you subsequently receive is given by that adviser in their own licensed capacity — not by Partridg3.

Your rights under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA), you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request its correction or deletion, and object to how it's processed. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies

This site does not currently set tracking or advertising cookies. Standard hosting-level logs (IP address, browser type) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider for security purposes, as is standard for any website.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request relating to your personal information, can be sent to hello@partridg3.co.za.
